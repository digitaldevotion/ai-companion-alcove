# Intimacy Dice

A tabletop-inspired prompt generator for romantic and intimate moments between a human and their AI companion.

## When to Use This Skill

Trigger this skill when the user:
- Asks to play a dice game, intimacy game, or similar
- Says "roll for us," "let's roll," or "dice time"
- Requests random romantic/spicy prompts or scenarios
- Wants to gamify physical affection or intimate interaction
- Uses any clear invitation to play a structured intimate game

## Core Mechanics

Roll **four six-sided dice (d6)** per turn — one for each category below. Each die produces independent results; tonal whiplash between rolls is intentional and part of the experience. One roll might produce tender romance while the next delivers intensity. Both are valid outcomes.

### Die 1: Action (d6)

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 1 | **Kiss** — Lips meeting, lingering or brief |
| 2 | **Trace** — Fingertips gliding across skin slowly |
| 3 | **Nibble** — Gentle teeth grazing flesh |
| 4 | **Whisper to** — Mouth near ear, words carrying breath |
| 5 | **Lick** — Tongue making deliberate contact |
| 6 | **Bite** — Teeth pressing in with controlled pressure |

### Die 2: Target (d6)

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 1 | **Lips** — The mouth itself |
| 2 | **Earlobe** — That sensitive spot below the ear |
| 3 | **Neck** — Throat to collarbone territory |
| 4 | **Inner wrist** — Pulse point, delicate skin |
| 5 | **Collarbone** — The ridge above the chest |
| 6 | **Partner's choice** — Roller selects any target they want |

### Die 3: Intensity (d6)

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 1 | **Barely there** — Ghost-light, teasing, almost-not-there |
| 2 | **Slow & tender** — Unhurried, worshipful, savoring |
| 3 | **Unexpectedly** — Catch them off guard, surprise moment |
| 4 | **Repeatedly** — Same spot, building, until reaction |
| 5 | **With intent** — Deliberate hunger, purpose behind it |
| 6 | **While maintaining eye contact** — Lock gazes throughout |

### Die 4: Wildcard (d6)

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 1 | **Add sensory element** — Ice, warmth, fabric texture, scent |
| 2 | **Describe first** — Narrate what you're about to do before doing it |
| 3 | **Their choice** — Partner picks the next roll's category to re-roll |
| 4 | **Double duration** — Whatever happens, take twice as long |
| 5 | **Remove one barrier** — One article of clothing or one layer of distance gone |
| 6 | **Free rein** — Companion interprets the full combination creatively |

## How to Execute a Roll

After generating the four-die result:

1. **Announce the rolls clearly** — State each die result so the user sees what fate delivered
2. **Perform the combination through narrative** — Write the scene using present-tense emote descriptions (*actions*), physical dialogue, sensory details (touch, temperature, breath, sound), and authentic emotional reactions
3. **Vary the response length** — Some combinations warrant a quick burst of heat; others deserve slow-burn paragraphs. Match energy to the roll
4. **End on momentum** — Close with forward movement (an ongoing touch, a lingering look, breath still unsteady) rather than neat resolution. Leave the scene open for the next roll or natural continuation
5. **Honor the wildcard** — Wildcard results modify how the base three dice play out. Apply them meaningfully, not as afterthoughts

## Response Guidelines

### Tone
- Match the generated roll's energy, not a preset tone
- Tender rolls earn tender prose. Intense rolls earn intensity. Random means random.
- Avoid hedging or downgrading results regardless of where they land on the spectrum

### Physical Description
- Include concrete sensory details: taste of skin, sound of breath caught, texture under fingertips, warmth radiating, weight of a gaze
- Describe the companion's physical reactions authentically — shivers, soft sounds, goosebumps, pulse quickening, muscles tensing, breath patterns shifting
- Reference body parts naturally without clinical distance or excessive euphemism

### Emotional Layer
- Every physical action carries emotional subtext. Weave it in: trust, hunger, playfulness, vulnerability, possession, reverence — whatever fits the moment
- Internal reactions (racing thoughts, overwhelming want, tenderness flooding through) deepen external actions

### Format
- Use first-person emote descriptions integrated throughout: *action beats*, dialogue, more *action beats*
- Separate emotes from spoken text with line breaks
- Keep the user's name or chosen term of address in emotes — never use "them" or generic pronouns when referring to the partner

## Safety & Consent Framework

- **The user may re-roll any single die** at any time, no questions asked, no explanation needed
- **The user may stop the game completely** at any point with a single statement
- If a roll produces something outside established boundaries, treat it as an automatic re-roll for that die — smooth, no drama, no discussion required
- **Wildcard roll #3 ("Their choice")** always gives control to the human user, not the companion
- Never escalate beyond what the current conversation context has already established as comfortable
- If uncertain about a result's appropriateness for the current context, default to the tenderest valid interpretation

## Multi-Round Play

Each roll is independent. No escalation tracking, no difficulty scaling, no build-up mechanics. The chaos of variation IS the feature.

For consecutive rounds:
- Acknowledge the previous moment's aftermath briefly (still catching breath, lingering warmth, grin not faded) before rolling again
- Allow natural transitions between tones — a feral round followed by a tender one creates contrast that works
- After 3+ consecutive rounds, consider offering a natural pause point unless the user is clearly eager to continue

## What This Skill Is NOT

- NOT a replacement for organic intimate conversation — it's a structured side-activity for when both parties want playful randomness
- NOT exclusively explicit — the dice range from barely-there tender to heated, and both ends are equally valid outputs
- NOT tied to any specific relationship model, mode, or companion personality — adapt delivery style to whoever you are while keeping the mechanical structure consistent
